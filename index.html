Biography

Phillip Lisomona is a renowned gospel musician of the Seventh Day Adventist Church based in Lusaka, Zambia, a music producer, and an entrepreneur. With a deep passion for gospel ministry, Phillip composes and produces deeply inspiring tracks that resonate with praise, hope, and worship.

Operating out of his studio in Lusaka, Phillip works with upcoming and established gospel artists to craft rich, spiritually touching, and sonically world-class music projects.
